User Scenarios for Each Page

First Page-Introduction
As a visitor,
I want to see an overview of the creators background,
So that I can have an idea of what the creator likes to play.

Second Page-Games
As a viewer,
I want to see what games the creator likes to play,
So that I will understand what the creator enjoys about the games mentioned.

Feedback Page
As a viewer,
I want to leave feedback and suggestions about the creators games,
So that they could potentially know what other games they could play.
